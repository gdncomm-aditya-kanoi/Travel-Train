This is **Day 2 of the [Himachal trip]({{ '/trips/manali/' | relative_url }})**, a day away from Manali, on a bike, to two places down the valley: the village of Naggar, with its castle and temples, and the small village of Jana, with its waterfall. I was back in Manali by the evening and returned the bike. The costs below are per person.

<div class="callout"><strong>The short version.</strong> Rent a bike in Manali, ride to Naggar, see the castle and the gallery, have cake, ride on to Jana for a home-cooked meal and the waterfall, and be back by evening.</div>

### Naggar

Naggar sits on the other side of the Beas from Manali. Its old castle is a 15th-century building of wood and stone, and from the carved balcony you look down the whole valley. The castle is open from 9 AM to 6 PM and the ticket is ₹50. Inside, the stairs and passages are dark and narrow, and the walls are carved.

### Cake at Naggar Delight Café

The Naggar Delight Café is a red-fronted bakery in the village with a coffee shop and homemade cakes. I had the black forest cake and a blueberry cheesecake, which were both good, and a cinnamon roll.

### Jana

Jana is a small hamlet above the Beas, reached by a narrow road through a deodar forest. It is quiet, with a few houses, apple orchards and a waterfall nearby. The road itself is half the trip: sheep, pine shade and almost no traffic.

We ate at Sagar Thakur Dhaba, which is a home kitchen: a woman cooks on a wood fire and serves a steel plate of brown rice, siddu (a steamed bread), dal, ghee and gud (jaggery). It was the best meal of the day.

### The waterfall and the apples

The Jana waterfall is a short walk from the village, with a small bridge over the stream. Apples were everywhere in late September, in the orchards and by the road.

### What I'd add to this plan

<div class="callout"><strong>A few things worth adding</strong>, from reading around rather than from my own trip, so check them locally:
<ul>
<li><strong>Fill the tank in Manali.</strong> There are few petrol pumps on this side road.</li>
<li><strong>Ask at the dhaba before you go.</strong> A home kitchen cooks to order, so tell them when you will get there.</li>
<li><strong>Wear shoes with grip</strong> for the steps down to the waterfall, which are wet.</li>
<li><strong>Leave Jana by 4 PM</strong> so the Manali road is not in the dark.</li>
</ul></div>
