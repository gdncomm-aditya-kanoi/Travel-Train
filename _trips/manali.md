Manali is the base for this whole trip. I came in on the Delhi bus before the Hampta Pass trek and came back through it after, so this page covers both visits. The next page is [Naggar and Jana]({{ '/trips/naggar-jana/' | relative_url }}), the day I spent on a bike. The costs below are per person.

<div class="callout"><strong>The short version.</strong> Overnight bus from Delhi, a dorm bed in Old Manali, Hidimba Devi in the morning, cafés by the river in the evening, a rented bike the next day. After the trek, a cheap hostel, the monastery and Sissu.</div>

### Getting there

I flew from Bengaluru to Delhi, then took an overnight bus from the Delhi ISBT to Manali. The bus was ₹3,597 for four of us, about ₹900 each. The road goes through Mandi and the Beas gorge. It is a long ride, so plan to do very little on the first morning. The map links for the route are in the plan below.

### Where I stayed

The Social Stays, in Old Manali, near the Manu Temple. We took a standard 6-bed mixed dorm with a balcony and a valley view. It is a room-only stay, and the two nights came to about ₹976 each (the group paid it as one booking and split it five ways). It is a short walk to Hidimba Devi, and the cafés are all around it. After the trek I took a cheaper Zostel bed in Old Manali for the night.

### Hidimba Devi and the old town

Hidimba Devi is a wooden temple of stacked roofs in the middle of a cedar forest, open from 8 AM to 6 PM with no ticket. I went in the morning, when the cedars were still in shade and few people were around. The ceiling and doors are carved with animals.

### After the trek: the monastery and Sissu

After the trek we got back to Manali in the afternoon and I took a cheap Zostel bed in Old Manali to rest. The next day I went to the Himalayan Nyingmapa Buddhist Monastery (6 AM to 7 PM, free), which has Tibetan masks and prayer flags, and then to Sissu Valley, which is on the road to Lahaul. A friend took the bus back to Delhi from here, and two of us went on to Barot, which is a separate page.

### What I'd add to this plan

<div class="callout"><strong>A few things worth adding</strong>, from reading around rather than from my own trip, so check them locally:
<ul>
<li><strong>Give the first day to the altitude.</strong> Manali is about 2,000 m, so a slow day after the night bus helps before any trek.</li>
<li><strong>Book the dorm early</strong> for late September and October, which is peak season.</li>
<li><strong>Carry cash.</strong> Some of the smaller dhabas and the parking places do not take cards.</li>
<li><strong>Check the Rohtang and Atal Tunnel permits</strong> if you plan to go beyond Sissu.</li>
</ul></div>
