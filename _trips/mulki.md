Mulki is a tiny, laid-back surf village near Mangalore, where mornings start with waves and evenings end with good food and new friends. This page is the parent of a 4-day coast trip with three friends: we based ourselves in Mulki the whole time, surfed there, and made two day trips from it, one south to [Mangalore]({{ '/trips/mangalore/' | relative_url }}) and one north to [Udupi]({{ '/trips/udupi/' | relative_url }}). Read this page first, then open the other two for the details of each day.

### How the three pages fit together

Mulki sits between the two cities: Mangalore is to the south and Udupi to the north, each about an hour away by road (check the exact distance on the map). So Mulki is the base, and the other two are day trips from it.

- **Mulki (this page):** the stay, the surfing, the pack list and the money.
- **[Mangalore]({{ '/trips/mangalore/' | relative_url }}) (Day 1):** because you arrive in the morning you miss the first surf session, so spend that day in the city with a rented bike.
- **[Udupi]({{ '/trips/udupi/' | relative_url }}) (Day 2):** surf first, then go north in the afternoon.
- **Day 3** stays in Mulki until 4 PM, and the bike goes back in Mangalore on the way to the train.

### Getting there

We took a night train from Bengaluru on 6 March and reached Mulki by the morning of 7 March. The ticket worked out to ₹366 each after cancellations and extras. The station sign is the first thing you see, and the beach is a short way from there.

### Where to stay and what it costs

We stayed with the surf school. The stay and surfing package cost **₹7,500 per person**. The stay sits by the backwaters, with a riverside deck for slow mornings, tents and hammocks for the evenings, and a couple of cats around. I did not note the exact name or address of the hostel, so check the school's Instagram, @mulki.in, for the latest.

### The surfing

Three sessions over the trip. We arrive on the morning of Day 1 and go to Mangalore, so we miss the first session; that is why Day 2 has two sessions back to back. The last session is on Day 3 before checkout. The board is provided by the surf school.

### The days

**Day 1: Mangalore.** We reached in the morning, freshened up, had brunch and took the bus to Mangalore to rent a bike and explore. Everything for that day is on the [Mangalore page]({{ '/trips/mangalore/' | relative_url }}). We came back to Mulki late at night.

**Day 2: two sessions, then Udupi.** Two sessions in the morning, then we got ready and went to Udupi. The places and the shortlist are on the [Udupi page]({{ '/trips/udupi/' | relative_url }}).

**Day 3: the last session, then home.** One more session, then checkout and a wander around Mulki. We left by 4 PM for Mangalore, returned the bike by 6 PM and took a local auto to the railway station for the train to Bengaluru. On Day 4 we reached Bengaluru and took a cab home.

### What to pack

The surf school provides the board. You bring the rest. This is my list, and what I carried myself:

- A pair of clothes, and swimwear for surfing
- A rash guard or surf tee, quick-dry shorts and a surf hat
- Sunscreen, a water bottle and slippers
- A towel, a bedsheet or shawl for the train, and a pair of shoes
- Driving licence and Aadhar card (you need the licence to rent the bike in Mangalore)
- A cap, toothbrush and toothpaste, skincare, and some emergency tablets

### What I'd add to this plan

<div class="callout"><strong>A few things worth adding</strong>, from reading around rather than from my own trip, so check them locally:
<ul>
<li><strong>Book early.</strong> A stay with surfing fills up on weekends, so confirm the dates before you buy the train ticket.</li>
<li><strong>Go with the instructor's advice on the water.</strong> Ask about the current and the tide before you go in.</li>
<li><strong>Re-apply sunscreen.</strong> The sun on the water is stronger than it feels, so use a rash guard.</li>
<li><strong>Keep your phone dry.</strong> Leave it at the stay or in a waterproof pouch during sessions.</li>
<li><strong>Carry cash.</strong> Small shops and autos may not take UPI.</li>
</ul></div>
