Chikmagalur is Karnataka's coffee country: hills the colour of freshly cut grass, clouds that sit right on the ridges, and waterfalls that only really run in the monsoon. I went for 3 days in August 2024 with a friend, took the train to Kadur and a bus up, stayed two nights at Manu Homestay and covered the peaks and waterfalls on a rented bike. I kept an itinerary, a price list and a story-song list for it, so I put them all here.

<div class="callout"><strong>The short version.</strong> Train from Bengaluru to Kadur, two nights at Manu Homestay, Mullayangiri and Hirekolale Lake on Day 1, Z Point and Hebbe Falls on Day 2, and Jhari Falls and Baba Budan Giri on Day 3 before the train home.</div>

### Getting there

I took the train from Bengaluru to Kadur and a bus on to Chikmagalur (₹45 each). Each train ticket was about ₹330 for two of us, and I had to cancel and rebook twice, so the tickets are not as clean as they look. The BMTC bus, a coffee at the station and the AC waiting room added a little more. From Kadur the bus took us the rest of the way. The tourist guide below shows how the roads and towns connect.

### Getting around

Rent a bike. Royal Brothers cost about ₹1,390 for the trip (we also lost a small booking fee when we changed plans), and we filled up the tank twice, ₹400 and ₹300. Most of the good places are on narrow hill roads, so a bike is far more fun than hiring a cab. Hebbe Falls and Jhari Falls both need a jeep at the end: ₹500 each and ₹165 each.

### Day 1: Mullayangiri, Hirekolale Lake and MG Park

Mullayangiri is the tallest peak in Karnataka and the main reason to come. There is a temple and a hill-top cave at the top. Maggi and coffee at the top cost ₹130 for two. After that go to Hirekolale Lake, about 9 km out, with hills and coffee estates around it, then a coffee stop and Mahatma Gandhi Park (₹80 for two), which has a hill view, a toy train and a musical fountain.

### Day 2: Z Point, Botanical Park and Hebbe Falls

Go up to Kemmangundi for Z Point (entry ₹50 and ₹160 security for the group) and the Sri Krishna Rajendra Botanical Park, which has a rose garden. Then take the jeep to Hebbe Falls (₹1,000 for two, security ₹20) and walk the rest of the way. We stopped for bhajji, coffee and a bathroom on the way, and ended the day with dinner.

### Day 3: Jhari Falls, Baba Budan Giri and the temple

Take the jeep to Jhari Falls (₹330 for two). Baba Budan Giri and the Shri Deviramma Bettada Temple are on the same stretch of hills. We had a bread omelette for breakfast and Maggi and coffee, corn, Boost and bhel on the way, and then a bus to Kadur, dinner and chai at the station before the train home to Bengaluru. The auto from the station to my place cost ₹50.
