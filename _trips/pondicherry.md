Pondicherry is a French colony on the Tamil coast, with yellow houses, quiet beaches and bakeries on every corner. I went for 3 days in March 2024 with friends, took the overnight bus from Bengaluru, rented a bike and spent most of my time on the road between Auroville and the sea. I kept a list of cafés, churches and beaches along the way, so I put them all here.

<div class="callout"><strong>The short version.</strong> Overnight bus from Bengaluru, two nights at Zostel Pondicherry, Auroville on Day 1, a boat to Paradise and Eden beaches on Day 2, and churches and White Town before the bus home on Day 3.</div>

### Getting there

Pondicherry is about 6 hours from Bengaluru. The best way is by road through Vellore, which is a little longer but scenic, and you can also take the overnight train. I took the overnight bus both ways: ₹1,890 up and ₹1,646 back for two of us.

### Where to stay

I stayed at Zostel Pondicherry, ₹3,580 for two of us for 2 nights. Do the web check-in before you go. If you are searching for a homestay or hotel, look outside White Town or near Auroville village: you will find breathtaking views, beach views and cheaper prices there. Zostel is a good option too.

### Day 1: Auroville

I picked up the bike, had a coffee at KBS Coffee Bar and croissants at Hot Breads, and rode to Auroville. Parking was ₹50. The Matrimandir is a one-time thing and you watch it from a distance. I had a cold coffee, a meal at La Boutique and a gelato, and finished with dinner at The Tadka Cafe.

### Day 2: boat to Paradise and Eden beaches

Go to the Chunnambar Boat House (parking and entry ₹84). The boat to Paradise Beach and Eden Beach costs ₹354 up and down. Paradise Beach is the best one, with blue water, but it has no changing room, so it is good for lunch and drinks. Eden Beach has a changing room and a bathing area and is one of the best-kept beaches. We had lunch at Escape In and ended the day with a waffle and a biryani dinner.

### Day 3: churches, White Town and home

Start with a coffee, then see the churches. Our Lady of Angels (Notre Dame des Anges) sits in White Town and is built in Greco-Roman style. It looks its best at Easter, and you should avoid Christmas, when it is too crowded. Add the Sacred Heart Basilica and the Manakula Vinayagar Temple, and Immaculate Conception Church if you have time. I had a bakery stop at Auroville Bakery and Hot Breads and a KSRTC lunch before heading home. The auto from SB to my place cost ₹142 for two.
