This is **Day 1 of the [Kudremukh trip]({{ '/trips/kudremukh/' | relative_url }})**. There is no direct bus from Bengaluru to Kalasa that suited us, so we went to Belur first, which is where you change. We had a few hours before the Kalasa bus, and we spent them on the Hoysala temple. The permit, the stay and the trek are on the [Kudremukh page]({{ '/trips/kudremukh/' | relative_url }}).

<div class="callout"><strong>The short version.</strong> Bus from Majestic to Belur, sugarcane juice, the temple, lunch, then the 3:15 PM Belur bus to Kalasa and an auto to the homestay.</div>

### Getting there

I went from HSR Layout to the Majestic bus stand, and took the bus to Belur. At Belur I had sugarcane juice before going to the temple, and left my shoes at the stand outside. After the temple, I had lunch before the afternoon bus.

### The temple

Belur is the Hoysala town, and the Chennakeshava temple is the reason to stop. The whole building is carved: the walls are layered with bands of elephants, riders and scenes, and every pillar and ceiling has something on it. The UNESCO mark on my cover photo is because the Hoysala temples are on the World Heritage list (from reading around, so check the details locally).

### Food

Sugarcane juice when we arrived, and a plain lunch in town before the bus. The costs are in the table below, and the rest of the food is on the [Kudremukh page]({{ '/trips/kudremukh/' | relative_url }}).

### Moving on to Kalasa

The Belur to Horanadu express buses stop at Kalasa on the way, via Mudigere and Kottigehara. They leave at 1:15, 2:15, 3:15, 4:00, 5:00 and 5:45 PM. We took the 3:15 PM, then had coffee and masala puri in Kalasa and an auto to Samse.

### What I'd add to this plan

<div class="callout"><strong>A few things worth adding</strong>, from reading around rather than from my own trip, so check them locally:
<ul>
<li><strong>Halebidu</strong> is the sister Hoysala temple, a short ride from Belur, if you have time for a second one.</li>
<li><strong>Check the temple timings</strong> and any closed hours in the afternoon before you plan lunch around them.</li>
<li><strong>Keep a buffer for the Kalasa bus.</strong> The last one is in the evening, and you still need the auto to the homestay.</li>
<li><strong>Dress for a temple</strong> and wear slippers you can take off easily.</li>
</ul></div>
