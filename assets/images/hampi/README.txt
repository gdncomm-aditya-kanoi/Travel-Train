ADD YOUR HAMPI PHOTOS HERE
1. Save photos in this folder (JPG, under ~1 MB each — squoosh.app shrinks them). Name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg, ...
2. Open _trips/hampi.md and list them:
     cover: /assets/images/hampi/01.jpg        (replaces the banner; optional)
     gallery:
       - /assets/images/hampi/02.jpg
       - /assets/images/hampi/03.jpg
3. Commit and push.
cover.svg is the city banner; regenerate it with: python3 tools/build_assets.py hampi
