Connect a Google Sheet, once (about 3 minutes)
  1. Create a new Google Sheet. Open Extensions, Apps Script, delete what is there and paste the code below.
  2. In the code, change CHANGE_ME to your password.
  3. Deploy, New deployment, Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone. Approve the permissions, then copy the Web app URL.
  4. Paste the URL here and press Save.

The PDF column takes a link, for example a Google Drive or Gmail link to the ticket or booking PDF, because a sheet cannot hold files. The password screen here is a light lock only. The real protection is the password inside your Apps Script, and keep the sheet itself private.
