**How to spend less and eat more.** Eat rice and curry or veg kottu at local eateries for ₹150 to ₹280 and use the saved cafés for coffee, smoothie bowls and dessert, which is about ₹850 a day on food. To ride a scooter you need an International Driving Permit with a two-wheeler endorsement and a local recognition permit (about LKR 3,000 to 5,000, or ₹850 to ₹1,400, from AA Ceylon in Colombo or your rental agency). For the Kandy to Ella train, book reserved 2nd or 3rd class seats early on the official Sri Lanka Railways site, or buy unreserved tickets at the station for under ₹150.

### SAARC entry prices

India is in SAARC, so for many sites in Sri Lanka we pay a lower rate than other foreigners, but only with the **original passport**. Photocopies and photos are not accepted at the ticket counters. The prices change, and the sources disagree, so treat every number below as an estimate and check the Central Cultural Fund e-ticketing site (eservices.ccf.gov.lk) before you go. The official fee page would not load for me, so these come from travel sites and the temple's own notice.
